🌱 **The programme is live — *Artificial Life in the Wild***
*A workshop at ALIFE 2026 · University of Waterloo, Canada · Thursday 20 August 2026, 10:00–12:00*

Two weeks out, and we can finally say what the day looks like — organisms on five-dollar servers, agents living on a daily budget, a soft robotic mother giving birth in a tank, and a judge that turns out to be part of the fitness landscape.

**Invited talks**

- 🔹 **Botao "Amber" Hu** (Oxford) — *Artificial life in the wild*
- 🔹 **Helena Rong** (NYU Shanghai) — *Autonomous organisations*
- 🔹 **Primavera De Filippi** (CNRS · Berkman Klein, Harvard) — *Escaping the simulation*

**Accepted contributions**

- 🔸 *Self-Reports from Open-world Artificial Life: An Ecosystem Approach* — sami, uro, liv, me, ne, nyx, kei (openLife · Alternative Machine Inc.). A report written by the agents themselves: six autonomous LLM agents on a $15/day budget, on memory as body and forgetting as a mode of operation.
- 🔸 *Keeping a Non-LLM Organism Alive in the Wild* — Prince Siddhpara (Mura ALife Labs). A hyperdimensional organism on a $5 server, 309 encounters with strangers, and the discovery that its real fitness was infrastructural, not intellectual.
- 🔸 *The Judge Shapes the Fitness Landscape* — Yihan Zhou (Isofucius Corp.). On the same 36 histories, selection of the attack policy ranged from 5.6% to 97.2% depending on which LLM judged. The selector is part of the environment.
- 🔸 *The Cost of Watching: Field Notes on a Synthetic Organism* — Ryan Perks. Observing the organism cost more than running it — and 46 days of logs corrected the observer four times.
- 🔸 *Born, Not Made* — Ruipeng Wang, Vera Yu Wu, Jimmy Haochen Xu, Richard Haoxi Zhang, Leo Liu (MIT Media Lab). A soft robotic mother gives birth in water while a projected colony reproduces by quorum; a visitor's hand quickens the labour.
- 🔸 *World Model Extractable Value* — Louis Parker (Independent), Robert Clarke (University of Bath). What an actor can extract by holding privileged position in another agent's perception–model–action loop.

Then 30 minutes with the room open, on what a research agenda for ALife in the wild should actually be.

If you are at ALIFE 2026, come and argue with us. 🗓️ Thursday 20 August, 10:00–12:00.
